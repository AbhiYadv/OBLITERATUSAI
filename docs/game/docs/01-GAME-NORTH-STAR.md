# Nexo World — Game North Star

## One-line pitch

**A third-person urban systems simulation where AI agents live as citizens, tools become shops, projects become districts, incidents physically disrupt the city, and the player restores the world by investigating evidence and making operational decisions.**

## Genre

- third-person urban simulation
- operational adventure
- simulation-based learning
- light systemic sandbox
- single-player browser game for the first release

## What we borrow from GTA-style games

- immediately readable third-person movement
- a city that continues moving around the player
- pedestrians and traffic
- walking to, entering, and driving vehicles
- clear spatial navigation
- physical places for activities instead of dashboard-only menus
- smooth transitions between exploration and missions

## What we reject

- crime fantasy
- weapons and combat in the prototype
- police and wanted systems
- copied missions, maps, UI, vehicles, characters, or art
- empty open-world scale
- content added only because GTA has it
- a generic driving sandbox without the Nexo identity

## Player fantasy

The player is an **Operator** entering a living digital city. Problems have locations, systems have owners, evidence can be gathered, tools can be visited, and progress changes the environment.

The player is not a floating camera, dashboard user, superhero, criminal avatar, robot, or all-powerful administrator.

## Core pillars

1. **The city is the interface.**
2. **Evidence before action.**
3. **Decisions have visible consequences.**
4. **AI agents are collaborators, not chat windows.**
5. **A polished district beats a giant empty map.**

## Core verbs

Walk, run, jump, observe, inspect, talk, ask, collect, compare, choose, drive, enter, exit, operate, validate, review.

## Core loop

1. Discover or receive a city problem.
2. Talk to the responsible agent.
3. Gather context and evidence.
4. Visit the required tools or locations.
5. Choose an intervention.
6. Validate the result.
7. Watch the city react.
8. Review the after-action report.
9. Unlock the next capability or district state.

## Session target

- exploration: 3–6 minutes
- investigation: 8–15 minutes
- intervention: 3–8 minutes
- validation and AAR: 3–5 minutes
- first vertical-slice target: 20–35 minutes

## First release boundaries

Included: one district, one finished mission, one drivable vehicle, pedestrian and traffic life, one operations building, one infrastructure destination, one tool row, evidence collection, AAR, and visible world-state restoration.

Deferred: combat, multiplayer, procedural full-city generation, a large economy, property ownership, dozens of vehicles, broad character customization, full day/night cycle, and weather simulation.
