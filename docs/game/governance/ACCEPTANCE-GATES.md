# Nexo World — Acceptance Gates

## Product
The pitch is clear, the mission supports it, and every feature belongs to a core pillar.

## Visual
Player, vehicle, NPCs, streets, buildings, and vegetation share one style. Reject toy cars, robots, cardboard facades, floating geometry, and sphere trees.

## Vehicle
Driver-side entry, door sequence, unchanged player, driving, collision, traffic awareness, safe exit, and nine approved QA views.

## Mission
Data-driven progression, evidence-gated best decision, recoverable wrong actions, explicit validation, visible city reaction, and explanatory AAR.

## Performance
Baseline and final measurements use the same device/browser. Standard tier targets 60 FPS; low tier remains playable at 30 FPS.

## Browser
Hard refresh, direct localhost input, tab-switch input clearing, readable load failures, and smoke tests across supported browsers.

## Vertical slice
A fresh player completes Meridian Signal Failure in 20–35 minutes without developer intervention.
